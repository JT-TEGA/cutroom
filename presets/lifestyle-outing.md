# Preset: lifestyle-outing
Outing / hangout vlog: a night out, brunch, a trip with friends.

- **Typical length:** 25–40s
- **Pacing:** quick cuts of moments, 0.6–1.5s each, cut every 2 beats when there's music. Talking bits keep their natural length.
- **Captions:** only on talking-to-camera parts.
- **Text overlays:** small location/time stickers ("Lekki, 7PM") when the place or time changes. Nothing else.
- **Music:** music leads. About 30% of moments keep their real sound (laughs, cheers, a toast) and the music dips under them.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts; a whip when the location changes.
- **Colour grade:** warm, light grain.
- **End card:** off.

## Structure
1. Hook (0–2s): the most energetic or best-looking moment of the day.
2. Arrival: first location sticker.
3. Moments in the order they happened; a new sticker + whip on each location change.
4. Ambient snippets on the funniest / loudest moments.
5. Close on a calm or golden moment (sunset, group shot, walking away).
