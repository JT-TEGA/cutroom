# Preset: shoot-mashup
High-energy video mashup of shoot footage.

- **Typical length:** 10–20s
- **Pacing:** hard cuts on the beat: every 2 beats in the build, every beat after the drop.
- **Captions:** off.
- **Text overlays:** none (optional one-word title).
- **Music:** music only, loud and punchy.
- **Transitions:** flash on downbeats and drops, the odd whip; speed ramps (fast into slow-mo) on drop shots.
- **Colour grade:** punchy.
- **End card:** on.

## Structure
1. Build (every 2 beats): looks and details.
2. Drop: flash, then every beat, ramps on the hero shots.
3. End on the strongest frame.
