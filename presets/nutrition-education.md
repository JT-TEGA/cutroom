# Preset: nutrition-education
Talking + text cards: teach something about food / macros.

- **Typical length:** 30–60s
- **Pacing:** slower than usual so text can be read: keep short breaths between points, cards stay up at least 2.5s.
- **Captions:** on (I'm talking); hidden while a full-screen card is up.
- **Text overlays:** key points as big bold text cards; macros / calories as on-screen cards ("Protein 40g").
- **Music:** low bed under my voice.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts.
- **Colour grade:** clean editorial.
- **End card:** on.

## Structure
1. Hook: the claim or question ("You're not eating enough protein").
2. Each point: me saying it + a key-point card; food B-roll with a macro card.
3. Recap card, then the end card.
