# Preset: transformation
Before / after.

- **Typical length:** 10–20s
- **Pacing:** build-up cuts on the beat (every 2 beats), each with its week/date label, then the reveal exactly on the beat drop.
- **Captions:** off (unless I talk).
- **Text overlays:** "Week 1" → "Week 12" style labels on every shot; before/after labels on the reveal.
- **Music:** music only. The track is started so the drop lands on the reveal.
- **Transitions:** cuts on the beat; flash on the drop.
- **Reveal:** swipe reveal (default) or split-screen, before on the left.
- **Colour grade:** punchy.
- **End card:** on.

## Structure
1. Hook: "Week 1" before shot, a hint of what's coming ("12 weeks. no days off.").
2. Build-up: progress shots in date order, every 2 beats, labels counting up.
3. Reveal on the drop: swipe or split, before vs after, labels on each side.
4. Hold the after shot for 2–3s.
