# Preset: photo-dump
Photos + short clips cut to the beat.

- **Typical length:** 10–20s
- **Pacing:** 0.3–0.8s per item, every change on a beat (1 or 2 beats, whichever lands in that range).
- **Captions:** off.
- **Text overlays:** optional one-line title at the start (small, clean, lowercase is fine), nothing else.
- **Music:** music only; clip sound muted.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts on the beat.
- **Colour grade:** soft film look, light grain so photos and clips match.
- **Photos:** light Ken Burns (slow zoom in/out or pan), landscape photos sit on a blurred fill.
- **End card:** off.

## Structure
1. Strongest image first (hook), optional title over it.
2. Everything else in capture order.
3. End on a favourite.
