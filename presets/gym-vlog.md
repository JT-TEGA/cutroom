# Preset: gym-vlog
Day in the life of a gym rat: training, meals, talking.

- **Typical length:** 45–75s
- **Pacing:** vlog pace; training clips 1–2s, talking at natural speed with silences cut.
- **Captions:** only on talking parts.
- **Text overlays:** time stamps for each part of the day ("5:30AM" + "pre-workout"); optional small macro card on meals.
- **Music:** bed under my voice, comes up on training montages.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts; whip between parts of the day.
- **Colour grade:** punchy (lighter than mashup).
- **End card:** off.

## Structure
1. Hook: best lift or a punchy line.
2. Time-stamped blocks in order (morning, pre-workout meal, training, post-workout meal, evening).
3. Training montages cut on the beat; talking over B-roll where I explain.
4. Short sign-off.
