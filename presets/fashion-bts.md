# Preset: fashion-bts
Behind the scenes of a shoot: raw, candid, handheld energy.

- **Typical length:** 20–40s
- **Pacing:** quick cuts, 0.5–1.2s. Shaky footage is fine here (it's the vibe), blurry footage is still skipped.
- **Captions:** only if I talk.
- **Text overlays:** minimal; at most a small sticker ("BTS", the shoot name).
- **Music:** real sound kept on every clip (shutter clicks, chatter), music sits under it.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts only.
- **Colour grade:** film, light grain, slight handheld drift on steady shots.
- **End card:** on.

## Structure
1. Cold open on a candid moment (laugh, flash firing).
2. Setup: lights, glam, styling.
3. Shooting moments, quickest cuts here.
4. Playback / reactions, wrap.
