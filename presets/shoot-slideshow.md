# Preset: shoot-slideshow
Final shoot photos as a slideshow.

- **Typical length:** 15–30s
- **Pacing:** beat-synced, every 2–4 beats (about 1–2s per photo).
- **Captions:** off.
- **Text overlays:** optional serif title at the start; optional credits at the end (photographer, stylist, MUA...).
- **Music:** music only.
- **Transitions:** clean cuts on the beat.
- **Colour grade:** clean editorial (no grain).
- **Photos:** slow push-ins only; landscape photos on a blurred fill.
- **End card:** off (credits close it instead).

## Structure
1. Hero photo first, optional title.
2. Photos in the order of the shoot (or best sequence by look/outfit).
3. Credits card on the last 2–3s if credits are given.
