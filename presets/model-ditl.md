# Preset: model-ditl
Day in the life of a model.

- **Typical length:** 45–75s
- **Pacing:** vlog pace. Talking parts at natural speed with silences cut; B-roll 1–2.5s.
- **Captions:** only on talking parts.
- **Text overlays:** a time stamp at the start of each part of the day ("6:30AM" + "call time"), top-left.
- **Music:** soft bed under my voice, comes up between talking parts.
- **Transitions:** hard cuts; whip between parts of the day.
- **Colour grade:** clean editorial.
- **End card:** off.

## Structure
1. Hook: best-looking shot + a teaser line ("come with me to a 6AM call time").
2. Time-stamped blocks in order (wake up, call time, glam, on set, wrap, home), each starting with a time stamp.
3. B-roll over talking where I explain something.
4. Short reflection to camera to close.
